# Third-party assets

## Kenney Car Kit

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- Author: Kenney
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Active model: `assets/cars/sedan-sports.glb`.
- The original external colormap texture was embedded into the GLB for portable import; geometry is unchanged. Godot may extract a local colormap during import.

Track centerlines were retained from the existing Apex Circuit prototype. Route names are descriptive; the game does not claim official licensing, laser scanning, or real circuit accuracy.

The visual direction references DREDGE (https://www.dredge.game/). No DREDGE models, textures, audio, logos, or other game assets are included.
